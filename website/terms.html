Using the software

Terms

Independent open-source software

gdocs-scope-mcp is independent open-source software. Its package metadata declares the MIT license. Review the repository's published license terms before use. It is not a Google product. Google services, GitHub hosting and your chosen MCP application or AI provider remain subject to their own terms.

Access and document changes

Use only accounts, documents and local files you are authorized to access. Review requested permissions before consenting. Document tools can modify or delete content; use your MCP application's approval controls and keep appropriate backups.

Availability

Availability of Google's APIs and the shared OAuth client may change. Public OAuth approval is pending; the shared client may require the project owner to add your account as a test user. Custom OAuth clients remain supported.

Support

Contact gdocs-scope-mcp@googlegroups.com. Do not send credentials, browser completion URLs or private document content to the support group.
